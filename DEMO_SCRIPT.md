# FIN SENTINEL — 2-minute demo script

**0:00–0:10 — Opening**  
“Meet FIN SENTINEL: an on-device financial early-warning system. Before you take your next loan, understand what it does to your financial future.”

**0:10–0:27 — Reconstruct**  
“On the left are realistic notifications from LazyPay, KreditBee, Amazon Pay Later, Slice and HDFC Bank. They arrive in English, Hinglish and Marathi, with different amount and date formats. FIN SENTINEL parses them locally — no lender backend and no credit bureau.”

**0:27–0:42 — Event graph**  
“Three messages can describe one loan: disbursal, reminder and payment. The event graph links those messages, removes duplicate counts, and turns 12 raw signals into five active financial obligations.”

**0:42–0:58 — Forecast**  
“The unified calendar shows the whole month at a glance: ₹1,500 on the 12th, ₹2,500 on the 18th, ₹3,000 on the 24th, plus the remaining commitments. Total existing repayment is ₹10,500 against ₹35,000 income.”

**0:58–1:20 — Simulate**  
“Now I test a proposed ₹50,000 loan. I move the estimated EMI slider to ₹4,500. The projected commitment becomes ₹15,000 and the repayment-to-income ratio rises to 42.9%.”

**1:20–1:38 — Protect and explain**  
“The threshold is 40%, so FIN SENTINEL raises a potential repayment stress warning. This is not a black-box score. Opening ‘Why am I seeing this?’ shows the exact income, existing commitments, proposed EMI and payment concentration that caused the warning.”

**1:38–1:52 — Multilingual access**  
“Finally, I ask: ‘Meri agli EMI kab hai?’ FIN SENTINEL answers in plain Hinglish: LazyPay, ₹1,500, due on 12 June.”

**1:52–2:00 — Close**  
“FIN SENTINEL moves borrowers from reactive debt tracking to proactive borrowing protection. Detect. Predict. Protect.”
